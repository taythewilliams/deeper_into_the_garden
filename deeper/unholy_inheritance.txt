despite the best wishes of my mothers
my dreamer
is incarcerated by the ignorance of my fathers

gifted and regifted is
the unholy inheritance 
of children who need more 
but can't ask for it 
who's only lot 
is to wake up and make it for themselves 
or ignore it
and pass on the chains 
which will bind the hopes and hopers 
the dreams and dreamers 
of generations to come
