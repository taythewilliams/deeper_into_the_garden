when you add two numbers
you are imagining what they could create together

when you subtract two numbers
you describe how far apart they live

when i was young i learned the word delta
it means subtraction
it means the difference between two things
it represents what it would take to go from one to the other

this word has always stuck with me

and i have always noticed a delta in my own body

there is what i am
there is what i know
and there is what i am aware of that i will soon know

each is so different
and the delta hurts
it is a weight that i carry

it is so difficult to watch myself live
so far below the standard that i wish i could live at
and even further from the standard that i know i will soo learn to have
